ABOUT MAKIVAN MIND CARE

Technology with a human touch.

Makivan Mind Care is an AI-assisted psychology and counselling platform founded by Psychologist Ivan Makhoba. We combine text and voice AI with professional oversight to help make psychological support more accessible and affordable.

Our AI is designed to assist — not replace "counsellors". Where additional attention is needed, conversations can be escalated for appropriate human review, keeping safety, privacy and professional judgement at the centre of the experience.

Starting in Kenya with a global vision, Makivan Mind Care is working to make supportive psychological services more accessible at an affordable cost. AI-assisted. Professionally guided. Human-centred.

OUR GOAL

Making psychological support easier to reach.

Our goal at Makivan Mind Care is to bridge the gap between people who need psychological support and the professionals who provide it.

We want to use technology responsibly to make counselling more accessible, affordable and timely, while keeping human expertise at the centre of care.
